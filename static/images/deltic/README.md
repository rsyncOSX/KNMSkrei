# T18-37K educational illustrations

Created 4 November 2026 for the KNM Skrei website with AI assistance. Current illustrations are drawn geometrically using Swift/CoreGraphics; initial AI-generated raster versions are retained as superseded references.

- `t18-37k-layout-v4.png`: current shared-instant schematic of A1/B1/C1, viewed from the free end. Exhaust angles relative to each piston's own TDC: B1 +10°, C1 +50°, A1 -30°; corresponding inlet angles -10°, +30°, -50°. Derived from manual Chapter 4 phase reference C1=0°, B1=-40°, A1=-80° and exhaust lead 20°. Harmonic travel model `170*(1-cos(angle))/2` in drawing units; not an exact slider-crank model. Identical 160 by 80 unit piston bodies. All ports closed, no flow arrows. Rotation arrows show AB/BC clockwise and CA anticlockwise. Source: `scripts/illustrations/deltic-layout.swift`.
- `t18-37k-layout-v3.png`: superseded airflow map with all three cylinders illustrated scavenging independently, not simultaneous engine timing.
- `t18-37k-layout-v2.png`: superseded geometric layout without gas-flow information.
- `t18-37k-layout.png`: superseded AI illustration with misleading connecting-rod geometry; not used by the website.
- `t18-37k-cycle-v3.png`: original geometric four-stage schematic. All eight piston symbols are generated by the same 110 by 96 unit drawing function; cylinders and port positions remain fixed. Source: `scripts/illustrations/deltic-cycle.swift`. Piston positions are illustrative, not calculated crank-angle positions.
- `t18-37k-cycle-v2.png`: superseded AI revision; piston lengths were inconsistent. Not used by the website.
- `t18-37k-cycle.png`: superseded first version; incorrect port flow depiction, not used by the website.

Technical basis: `content/en/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md`, Introduction (identification and leading particulars), Chapter 1 (general description), and Chapter 4 (phasing and timing).

The supplied website screenshots were subject references. These images use new compositions rather than tracing the historical illustrations. No claim of copyright clearance or exclusive ownership is made.

Suggested alt text:

- Layout: No. 1 cylinders at one shared instant: B1 near the end of compression, A1 compressing and C1 expanding. Inlet and exhaust ports alternate around the triangle and are all closed here. Crankshafts AB and BC rotate clockwise; CA rotates anticlockwise, viewed from the free end.
- Cycle: Four stages show opposed pistons compressing air, fuel combustion driving them apart, exhaust ports opening first, and blower air scavenging the cylinder through inlet and exhaust ports.

These are educational schematics, not maintenance or timing-setting drawings.
