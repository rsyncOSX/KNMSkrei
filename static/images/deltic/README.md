# T18-37K educational illustrations

- `t18-37k-phasing-verified.svg` shows cylinders A1, B1 and C1 from the free end at C1 exhaust top dead centre. The schematic labels the shared crankpins, the direction of shaft rotation and the angles of all six pistons. All six port belts are closed at this instant.
- `t18-37k-cycle-verified.svg` shows five cylinder states across one two-stroke cycle and compares the inlet port timing in the handbook and the 1967 Napier course notes.

The illustrations cite handbook figures 1.8 and 1.9 and figure 4.2 of the Napier course notes. The course notes are transcribed in `content/en/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md`.

Both SVGs are used on the restoration documentation landing page at `content/en/docs/_index.md`.
