# About the restoration

The restoration of KNM Skrei is one of several projects undertaken by [Marinens Veteranskip](https://www.marinensveteranskip.no). This website documents the restoration of her engine department as part of the wider effort to make her seaworthy again.

My name is Thomas, and I am a volunteer coordinating the work to prepare the engine department for sea trials as part of the effort to return KNM Skrei to sea.

If you have any comments or suggestions about this website, please feel free to contact me at thomeven@gmail.com.

## Background

My background spans naval engineering and IT, with service in the Royal Norwegian Navy followed by IT management roles in banking and higher education. I retired in 2022.

- **1979–1983:** Royal Norwegian Naval Academy (SKSK).
- **1983–1984:** Workshop service at BMV Laksevåg.
- **1984–1987:** Sea service as First Engineer and Chief Engineer with the 21st and 26th Motor Torpedo Boat Squadrons, respectively.
- **1987–1990:** Civilian employment.
- **1990–1992:** Royal Norwegian Navy; studying computer science at the University of Bergen. Awarded a master’s degree (cand.scient.) in 1993.
- **1992–1996:** IT projects in the Joint Staff at Defence Command Norway (FO), Huseby.
- **1996–2008:** Head of an IT department at DNB.
- **2008–2014:** IT Director at the University of Bergen.
- **2014–2017:** Deputy Director of IT at the University of Oslo.
- **2017–2022:** Head of IT at the University of South-Eastern Norway.
- **2022–present:** Retired.

## About this website

Documentation is written in English to support collaboration with the Napier heritage community and to make the experience accessible to other preservation projects.

This website is at an early stage. Photographs and background references are available now; the project log will grow as work proceeds.
