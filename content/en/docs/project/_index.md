The engine department project will establish the condition and history of Skrei’s machinery, define the restoration work and document its verification through to commissioning and sea trials.

This is a proposed work scope, not a report of completed inspections or approval to operate machinery. Equipment listed below must be confirmed aboard Skrei; not every system will necessarily be fitted. Record observations, proposed work and completed work separately.

- [Compressed-air system](compressed-air/): starting-air supplies, component surveys and verification.
- [Auxiliary machinery](auxiliary-machinery/): auxiliary engines, HANSA generators and associated equipment.
- [Main engines]({{< relref "/docs/main-engines/_index.md" >}}): Napier Deltic T18-37K identification and machinery records.
- [Engine department work scope](engine-department/): machinery, supporting systems and safety equipment to survey and restore.
- [Baseline, planning and verification](planning-and-verification/): equipment records, history, resources, decision points and acceptance evidence.

## Technical reference now available

The [illustrated Napier Deltic T18-37K course notes]({{< relref "/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md" >}}) provide a searchable reference for the main-engine work. Use Chapters 1–12 to identify assemblies and trace systems, [Chapter 13]({{< relref "/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md" >}}#chapter-13) when preparing operating checks, [Chapter 14]({{< relref "/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md" >}}#chapter-14) for the maintenance plan and [Chapter 15]({{< relref "/docs/T18_37K/T1837K.md" >}}#chapter-15) for unit-replacement planning. Each chapter links to the original PDF pages.

These are instruction-school notes, revised December 1967. Their front matter names the **Handbook for Napier Deltic Engines, Publication 500 Series**, as the technical authority. Record which instructions apply to each installed engine and modification standard before adopting work procedures or limits. The notes do not cover the auxiliary engines or establish the condition of Skrei’s machinery.

Add dated project-log entries as work proceeds, linking each entry to the relevant system, equipment record, photographs and inspection or test results.
