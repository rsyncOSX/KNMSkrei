This collection presents the **Deltic Engine Type T18-37K** course notes issued by **D. Napier & Son Limited, Service Department Instruction School**. The source contents page records the latest revision as **December 1967**. The material is provided here as a historical technical reference alongside the [records of Skrei’s main engines]({{< relref "/docs/main-engines/_index.md" >}}).

## About the engine and the notes

The source introduction describes the Deltic as a water-cooled, two-stroke compression-ignition engine with opposed pistons. Three cylinder banks form a triangle, with a crankshaft at each corner. The T18-37K notes cover the engine’s construction, turbo-blower, drive arrangements and bi-directional gearbox, followed by lubrication, cooling, fuel, controls and starting systems. The final chapters cover operation, servicing schedules and repair by unit replacement.

The original notes accompanied a lecture course for students at Napier’s instruction school. They identify the **Handbook for Napier Deltic Engines, Publication 500 Series**, as the authority for technical questions. This distinction is retained in the [transcribed front matter]({{< relref "T1837K.md" >}}#front-matter-and-introduction).

## Using this edition

The supplied transcription is marked as a proofread working draft dated **4 November 2026**. It retains the original chapter order and includes illustrations, searchable figure keys, tables and numbered procedures. Source notes identify apparent omissions and significant typographical errors in the scans.

References use **PDF page numbers counted from 1**, which can differ from the printed page numbers because some illustration pages were unnumbered. Chapters 11 and 15 each span two PDFs. The original scans remain available below for comparison with the transcription.
