This site documents the engine department restoration of KNM Skrei. It is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [Docsy theme](https://www.docsy.dev/).

Documentation is in English. Historical background pages cite their sources; project findings will be recorded separately as restoration work proceeds. External references provide context and do not replace the applicable engine manuals or approved working procedures.
